About

Navigating the web safely is becoming harder. Modern scammers have traded simple tricks for advanced technical deception, creating fake links that look real enough to fool even the most cautious eye. We use a mix of smart pattern detection and official security databases to check every link. Here is exactly what we look for:

Our system combines intelligent pattern detection with trusted security databases to evaluate every URL before you interact with it. Below is exactly what we analyze:

  • 🔎 Typosquatting Detection: Identifies look-alike attacks such as netfl1x or amaz0n.
  • ➖ Dash-Stuffing Analysis: Flags domains overloaded with hyphens designed to mimic real URLs.
  • 📏 Suspicious Subdomain Length: Detects long subdomains that hide malicious domains on mobile screens.
  • 🔐 URL Obfuscation Checks: Analyzes encoded characters and unusual ports used to bypass firewalls.
  • ⚠️ High-Risk TLD Filtering: Flags risky extensions like .xyz, .top, and .zip.
  • 🌐 Google Safe Browsing Integration: Cross-checks URLs against Google’s real-time global threat database.
